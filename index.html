This Isn't a Template.
This Is a Methodology.

Most people don't know that federal law — the Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA) — gives every consumer the right to an accurate credit report, and a defined legal process to challenge what isn't. Most "credit repair" companies charge hundreds of dollars a month to walk that same process on your behalf.

The NorthStar Credit OS teaches you that process directly: how credit bureaus are required to investigate disputes, what "verification" legally means under §611, when you're entitled to escalate under §623, and how to build your own case file — step by step, using Claude AI as your guide.

This is a self-directed learning system. You review your own report, you write your own letters using our templates and instructions, and you send them yourself. Nothing is filed or submitted on your behalf.
